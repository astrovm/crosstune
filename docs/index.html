Crosstune
Features

Open any music link in your favorite app.

Got a link from one service but listen on another? Tap it and the same song opens where you listen.

Download for Android

Free, open source and ad-free. Works on Android 8 or newer.

What it does

Tap and go

Links open straight in your favorite app.

Exact match

Supported apps open the song itself instead of a search.

Your rules

Pick an app per service, or ask each time.

Any app

Add any app or site with a search page.

Shortcuts

One-tap paste, recent links, Quick Settings tile.

Private

No account. History stays on your phone.
