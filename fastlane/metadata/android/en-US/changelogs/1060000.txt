Select a link in any app and pick Crosstune from the menu to open it.

Long-press the Crosstune icon for your latest songs, and share links straight to an app from the share sheet.

When you share a converted link, you can also copy or share the original one.

Setup is clearer: pick where you listen first, and stop other apps before allowing the links. A link shared from the app you listen in no longer opens straight back there.
