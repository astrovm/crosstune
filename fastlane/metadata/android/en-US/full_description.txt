Open any link. Name any song. Follow every word.

Someone sends you a link from one music service, but you listen on another. Tap the link and the same song opens in your favorite app. Spotify to YouTube Music, Apple Music to Deezer, TIDAL to Spotify: mix them however you like. Works with songs, albums, artists and playlists.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Reads links from Spotify, YouTube Music, YouTube, Apple Music, Deezer, TIDAL, SoundCloud, Bandcamp and Audiomack, and opens them in any of those or in Amazon Music and Qobuz.</li>
<li>Playlists and albums show their songs: open any one in the music app you picked, copy or share the list, or play it all, in order or shuffled, in YouTube Music, YouTube or TIDAL.</li>
<li>Send each service somewhere different, like YouTube links to YouTube Music and everything else to Apple Music.</li>
<li>Show in Crosstune first: the song plays in the music app you picked while you stay.</li>
<li>Apple Music, Deezer, YouTube Music, TIDAL, SoundCloud and Audiomack can open the song, album or artist itself instead of a search, Bandcamp and Qobuz the song or album, and YouTube the song.</li>
<li>Add other apps and sites that have a search page.</li>
<li>Paste with one tap, search and reopen recent links, or open a copied link from the home screen widget, the Quick Settings tile, or by long-pressing the app icon.</li>
<li>Names a song playing nearby: Crosstune listens itself, or opens Shazam or Google's song search. Also opens Shazam links and songs shared from Pixel Now Playing.</li>
<li>Can't read a link? Open it in the app it came from, copy the search text, or share a search link.</li>
<li>Find a song by typing its name instead of a link. Play keeps you in Crosstune: a bar at the bottom shows what's playing, its player has up next, repeat, like and a sleep timer, and you swipe between player and lyrics.</li>
<li>Lyrics in time: they follow your music app or listen along, float over other apps, and play with MilkDrop visuals behind them. Wrong words or timing? Report them from the lyrics in one tap: the song and its timing go privately to the developer.</li>
<li>Learn the words with kana, pinyin, romaji and any other script in Latin letters, and translations for the languages you don't already read. Choose what appears on each side of your practice cards. Make more than one kind of card from a line, each on its own schedule.</li>
<li>Sign in with your paid ChatGPT plan to translate whole songs, explain lines, find words worth learning, and find songs by describing them.</li>
<li>Runs on Android TV too, with the remote.</li>
<li>Material You colors on Android 12 and later.</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
No accounts, analytics or ads. History stays on your device. To show a link's title, artist and cover, Crosstune only asks the service the link comes from. To open the song itself, the title and artist also go to the search of Apple, Deezer, YouTube Music, YouTube or Bandcamp. Play all and Shuffle send the songs' titles and artists to YouTube Music's search, and the videos found to YouTube. To name a song, Crosstune uses the microphone only while listening, and sends Shazam just a fingerprint of the sound, never the audio. Typing a song's name sends it to Deezer's and Apple's song search, and asking for lyrics or chords sends the song's title and artist to lyrics and chord sites (LRCLIB, YouTube Music, NetEase, Letras, Ultimate Guitar, Cifra Club, LaCuerda, U-FRET). Translations go to MyMemory. Signed in with ChatGPT, the AI features send the song's title, artist and lyrics, and what you ask, to OpenAI. When microphone access is already allowed, lyrics can briefly listen to correct and remember the intro timing for that recording. They also line up to the singing, heard on the phone and never sent.
