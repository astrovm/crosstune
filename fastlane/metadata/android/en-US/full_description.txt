Crosstune opens music links from one service in the app you actually use.

Share, paste or tap a link to a song, album, artist or playlist from Spotify, YouTube Music, YouTube, Apple Music, Deezer, TIDAL, SoundCloud or Bandcamp, and Crosstune opens the same thing in any of them, or in Amazon Music.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Understands links, short links and URIs from eight services.</li>
<li>Opens a result, or a link it couldn't read, in the app it came from.</li>
<li>Choose which services' links Crosstune intercepts, and where each service's links go.</li>
<li>Add custom destinations for any app or website with a search URL.</li>
<li>Choose a default destination, or have Crosstune ask where to open each shared link.</li>
<li>Optional exact matches: for Apple Music and Deezer, Crosstune can look the item up with their public search APIs and open it directly instead of a search.</li>
<li>Quick Settings tile and launcher shortcut that open whatever music link you copied.</li>
<li>Recent history, so you can re-open or re-share a link.</li>
<li>Copy the search text or share a search link.</li>
<li>Material You colors on Android 12 and later.</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
Crosstune has no accounts, analytics or ads. It only contacts the service a link belongs to, through its public page or official key-less API, to read the title and artist. With exact matches turned on, it also sends that title and artist to Apple's or Deezer's search API when you open in those apps. History stays on your device.
