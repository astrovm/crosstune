Open any link. Follow every word.

Someone sends you a link from one music service, but you listen on another. Tap the link and the same song opens in your favorite app. Spotify to YouTube Music, Apple Music to Deezer, TIDAL to Spotify: mix them however you like. Works with songs, albums, artists and playlists.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Reads links from Spotify, YouTube Music, YouTube, Apple Music, Deezer, TIDAL, SoundCloud, Bandcamp and Audiomack, and opens them in any of those or in Amazon Music, Qobuz and Presto Music.</li>
<li>Playlists and albums show their songs: open any one in the music app you picked, copy or share the list, or play it all, in order or shuffled, in YouTube Music, YouTube, TIDAL or Spotify.</li>
<li>Send each service somewhere different, like YouTube links to YouTube Music and everything else to Apple Music.</li>
<li>Show in Crosstune first: the song plays in the music app you picked while you stay.</li>
<li>Apple Music, Deezer, YouTube Music, TIDAL, SoundCloud and Audiomack can open the song, album or artist itself instead of a search, Qobuz the song or album, Bandcamp the same, or a search in its app, and YouTube the song.</li>
<li>Add other apps and sites that have a search page.</li>
<li>Paste with one tap, search and reopen recent links, or open a copied link from the home screen widget, the Quick Settings tile, or by long-pressing the app icon.</li>
<li>Names a song played nearby, or by an app that doesn't name it: it listens, or opens Shazam or Google's song search. Also opens Shazam links and Pixel Now Playing shares.</li>
<li>Can't read a link? Open it in the app it came from, copy the search text, or share a search link.</li>
<li>Find a song by typing its name instead of a link. Play keeps you in Crosstune: a bar at the bottom shows what's playing, its player has up next, repeat, like and a sleep timer, and you swipe between player and lyrics.</li>
<li>Lyrics in time: they follow your music app or listen along, float over other apps, and play with MilkDrop visuals behind them. Wrong words, timing or reading? Report it in one tap, from the lyrics or on the word: it goes privately to the developer.</li>
<li>Learn the words with kana, pinyin, romaji and any other script in Latin letters, and translations for the languages you don't already read. Choose what appears on each side of your practice cards. Make more than one kind of card from a line, each on its own schedule. A daily reminder brings back the cards that are due.</li>
<li>Sign in with your paid ChatGPT plan to translate whole songs, explain lines, find words worth learning, and find songs by describing them.</li>
<li>Runs on Android TV too, with the remote.</li>
<li>Material You colors on Android 12 and later.</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
No accounts, analytics or ads. History stays on your device. Naming a song sends Shazam a fingerprint of the sound, never the audio, and lyrics line up with the singing on your device. A song's title and artist go to the music services that find and open it, and to lyrics, chord and translation sites. Signed in with ChatGPT, AI features send OpenAI the song, its lyrics and what you ask. Crosstune's only server gets the problem reports you send, and automatic ones if you turn them on. It keeps no IP address and deletes each report after 180 days. Every service, and what each report carries: https://crosstune.cc/privacy/
